A make.fun entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty.

## What it does

`main.ts` writes a two-line warning to the writer's past self into `entry.txt`:

- **Line 1** warns about one specific moment: answering "I did" when the principal asks who designed the library mural, after Maya goes home.
- **Line 2** says why that moment mattered: the principal took the writer at their word and put the writer's name on the plaque, so Maya spent the rest of the year walking past her own work credited to someone else.

The entry is 228 characters (98 + 1 newline + 129), inside the 1–280 character gate.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes `entry.txt` to `process.env.MAKEFUN_OUTPUT` (default `/output`).
